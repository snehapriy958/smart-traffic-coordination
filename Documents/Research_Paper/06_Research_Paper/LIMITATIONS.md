# Section VII: Limitations & Scope Boundaries

1. **Simulation-Based Study**: Evaluated in microscopic cyber-physical simulation (Eclipse SUMO); has not undergone live field deployment on physical signal controllers.
2. **Synthetic Demand Configuration**: Demand reflects realistic heterogeneous ratios but has not undergone fine-grained calibration against live loop detector time series.
3. **Linear Arterial Geometry**: Demonstrates performance on a 3-junction linear arterial corridor; generalization to complex 2D grid networks requires further study.
4. **Volumetric Fuel Index**: Fuel consumption is reported as a petrol-equivalent volumetric index ($\rho_{\text{ref}} = 0.750\text{ kg/L}$) to normalize mixed petrol and diesel vehicles.
