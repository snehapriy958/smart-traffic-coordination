# Section IV: Proposed Methodology & Safe Control Architecture

The platform architecture couples SUMO with a real-time TraCI telemetry pipeline, an AI decision engine, and an immutable runtime safety shield:
1. **Telemetry & Feature Extraction**: Computes approach queue lengths ($v \le 0.1\text{ m/s}$), velocities, and vehicle modal states every second.
2. **AI Action Selection**: Every 5.0s, candidate actions $a_i \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ are generated by Fixed-Time, Coordinated Adaptive, Dueling Double-DQN, or Multi-Agent PPO.
3. **Safety Shield Interception**: Enforces $g_{\min} \ge 15\text{s}$, $g_{\max} \le 60\text{s}$, $y = 4\text{s}$, $r = 2\text{s}$, $T_{\text{red}} \le 120\text{s}$, and emergency preemption.
