# Paper Formatting & Style Guide

- **Format**: IEEE Transactions double-column format (`IEEEtran.cls`) or Elsevier single/double-column (`elsarticle.cls`).
- **Font**: Times New Roman 10pt (IEEEtran) or Computer Modern.
- **Bibliography Style**: `IEEEtran.bst` or `elsarticle-num.bst`.
- **Mathematical Equations**: Standard LaTeX math environments with equation numbering.
- **Figures**: Vector format (PDF or EPS) at 300+ DPI.
- **Tables**: LaTeX `booktabs` package with formal top, middle, and bottom rules.
